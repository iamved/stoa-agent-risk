Solutions · Stoa Agentic Risk Underwriter

The evidence becomes a submission your broker can actually place.

The Underwriting Engine turns your Stoa scan and stress-test evidence into a carrier-ready packet — every answer pre-filled, and every answer carrying the exact source it came from. It is the Assurance step of the scan pipeline: the point where findings become an application an underwriter can price.

Scan→ Registry→ Graph→ Stress test→ Assurance

How it works

Read the evidence in. Hand a signed packet out.

No re-keying a 60-question application by hand. The engine maps one canonical submission to each carrier's format, and flags exactly what it cannot verify for you.

01

Evidence in

Your scan registry and stress-test results become one canonical submission — capabilities, autonomy, data access, simulated performance.

02

Mapped to carriers

Declarative templates render posture-style and performance-style questionnaires. Adding a new carrier is one config file, not new code.

03

Signed packet out

Clean PDFs plus a performance workbook, a gap list of what still needs your attestation, and a hashed audit manifest.

The underwriting contract

  • Provenance on every line. Each answer keeps its source ref, confidence, and scan hash.
  • Unknown is never a “no”. Missing evidence is labeled, so nobody over-claims a posture that wasn't observed.
  • Your officers sign. Stoa reports evidence and its confidence; it does not attest for you or submit anything anywhere.
